should there be a distinction between templates and mediatypes (and mimetypes).
Mimetypes being the file types assigned by the mime-types package
Mediatypes being the media types, that the templates use to decide what is what. Mediatypes are basically the same given what is inside the folder.
Templates being the html visual representation, which can be customized. Templates change depending on how many files there are in a folder.
